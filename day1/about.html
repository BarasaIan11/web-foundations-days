About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture ideas, tasks, and reminders as soon as they occur to you. It helps you keep your thoughts organized so you can return to them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea or task into the new-note field.
  2. Select Add Note to save it to your list.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a reminder.

Features

  • Quick note capture
  • A clear list of saved notes
  • A simple, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add a new note
